<p><b>Why do Greek moving companies sound "metaphoric"?</b></p>
<p>Because <i>metaphor</i> never stopped meaning what it first meant: carrying something from one place to another. This bilingual English–Chinese lesson unpacks a single word and shows how it unlocks a whole family of English vocabulary, from <i>transfer</i> and <i>suffer</i> to <i>euphoria</i> and <i>semaphore</i>. You will discover why <i>metaphor</i> and <i>translate</i> are twins, why the "meta" in <i>metaphor</i> has nothing to do with being "meta," how Aristotle and Quintilian defined metaphor, and why English and Chinese both talk about arguments as if they were wars. Every section appears in English followed by Traditional Chinese, so you can read in either language or both.</p>
<p>為什麼希臘的搬家公司聽起來像「隱喻公司」？因為 metaphor 從來沒有忘記它最初的意思：把東西從一個地方搬到另一個地方。這堂中英雙語詞源課拆解一個字，帶你解鎖一整串英文單字，從 transfer、suffer 到 euphoria、semaphore。你會知道 metaphor 和 translate 為什麼是雙胞胎、metaphor 裡的 meta 為什麼跟網路上的「很 meta」無關、Aristotle 和 Quintilian 怎麼定義隱喻，以及為什麼中英文都把辯論說得像打仗。每一節英文在前、繁體中文在後，可以單讀一種語言，也可以對照閱讀。</p>
<h4>What you will gain｜你會學到</h4>
<ul>
<li>Decode more than a dozen English words from one root｜用一個詞根看懂十多個英文字</li>

<li>See the hidden metaphors English and Chinese share｜看見中英文共有的隱藏隱喻</li>
<li>Check yourself with a 4-question bilingual quiz｜用 4 題雙語小測驗自我檢查</li>
</ul>
