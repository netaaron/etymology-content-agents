| 2:09 | 第二條枝（陶土色）：transfer, refer, offer, prefer, suffer | Road two, Latin. *Transfer*: carry across. *Refer*: carry back. *Offer*: carry toward. *Prefer*: carry to the front. *Suffer*: carry from underneath. | 第二條路，拉丁。*Transfer*：搬過去。*Refer*：帶回來。*Offer*：帶到面前。*Prefer*：搬到最前面。*Suffer*：在底下扛著。 |
| 2:20 | 第三條枝（橄欖綠）：bear, birth, burden | Road three goes straight into Old English: *bear*, *birth*, and *burden*. A burden is, literally, what is carried. | 第三條路直接進入古英語：*bear*、*birth*、*burden*。Burden 的字面意思，就是被扛著的東西。 |
