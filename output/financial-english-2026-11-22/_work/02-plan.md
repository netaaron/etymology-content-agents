## 0. 核心訊息

- **EN**：Put financial English in historical order and it tells the story of money itself: from cattle you could count, to a bench you could sit at, to a promise you have to believe.

- **系列一致性**：沿用 metaphor 一集的視覺風格關鍵字，讓系列看起來是同一套。
