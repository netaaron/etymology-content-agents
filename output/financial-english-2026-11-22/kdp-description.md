<p>Because financial English remembers where money came from. Put words like <i>fee</i>, <i>capital</i>, <i>money</i>, <i>bank</i>, <i>credit</i>, <i>mortgage</i> and <i>finance</i> in historical order, and they tell the story of money itself: from cattle you could count, to coins struck beside a Roman temple, to a money-dealer's bench, to a promise you have to believe. Every section appears in English followed by Traditional Chinese, with parallels from Chinese characters such as 貝, 本 and 信.</p>
